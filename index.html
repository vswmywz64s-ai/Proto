Edinburgh's Most-Loved Victorian B&B

Allison House Hotel occupies an elegant Victorian townhouse in the leafy Mayfield Gardens neighbourhood — just 10 minutes from Edinburgh's Old Town, Royal Mile, and Arthur's Seat. Our 11 beautifully appointed en-suite bedrooms blend period charm with modern comforts.

Every morning starts with a legendary Scottish breakfast: locally sourced eggs, Stornoway black pudding, fresh baked breads, and a selection of homemade preserves.
